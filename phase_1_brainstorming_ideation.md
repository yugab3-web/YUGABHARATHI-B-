# Phase 1 – Brainstorming & Ideation

## Problem
Students may want a simple way to organize a healthy and enjoyable daily activity routine based on their available time and preferences.

## Idea
Build an AI-powered assistant that generates a simple 7-day wellbeing routine from user inputs.

## Proposed solution
FitBuddy collects a general goal, experience level, available time, and preferences. A carefully written prompt is sent to Gemini, which generates a structured routine.

## Target users
Students and general users who want a simple activity routine.

## Key features
- Goal selection
- Experience-level selection
- Available-time selection
- Preference input
- AI-generated 7-day routine
- Simple web interface

## Innovation
The project combines a simple form interface with prompt engineering and Generative AI to create personalized text output.

## Safety consideration
The project focuses on general wellbeing and avoids weight-loss targets, calorie restriction, body-shape goals, and unsafe exercise.
